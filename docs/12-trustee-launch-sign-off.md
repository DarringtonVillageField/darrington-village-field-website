# Trustee Launch Sign-off

## Darrington Village Field Trust Website

**Status:** Awaiting trustee review  
**Prepared:** 24 July 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This checklist records the trustee decisions required before the new website is made publicly available at `darringtonvillagefield.org.uk`.

The website remains under development and Netlify automatic publishing remains locked while approval is obtained.

---

## 2. Documents for approval

### Privacy Notice

Review the website page at `/privacy/` and confirm whether it accurately describes:

- the personal information the Trust may receive;
- why the Trust uses that information;
- the people and service providers that may process it;
- the use and publication of photographs;
- the absence of intentional non-essential analytics or advertising cookies in the first release;
- how people can exercise their data-protection rights; and
- how people can contact the Trust or the Information Commissioner's Office.

The trustees should also agree:

- a proportionate approach to deleting routine enquiries when they are no longer needed;
- which financial, safeguarding, contractual and governance records need longer retention;
- who will carry out and record periodic reviews;
- that the terms and data-processing arrangements for Netlify, Cloudinary, GitHub and the Trust's email providers have been considered; and
- that approved photographs and public website material must not be treated as storage for private or sensitive records.

### Accessibility Statement

Review the website page at `/accessibility/` and confirm whether it accurately describes:

- the Trust's WCAG 2.2 Level AA target;
- the accessibility measures built into the website;
- how visitors can request help or an alternative format;
- how visitors can report an accessibility problem;
- the testing completed before launch; and
- any remaining limitations recorded after the final checks.

The statement will be updated after the final keyboard, zoom, responsive and VoiceOver checks. It should not claim full compliance unless the evidence supports that claim.

---

## 3. Approval record

The trustees should record:

- the date the Privacy Notice was approved;
- the date the Accessibility Statement was approved;
- any amendments requested;
- the names of the trustees participating in the decision;
- the agreed review date; and
- who is responsible for arranging the next review.

A review should also be triggered by a significant change to the website, its service providers, its use of personal information, or its interactive features.

---

## 4. Items not requiring policy approval

The following operational information has been confirmed:

- Dogs are allowed on the Field, including during events.
- Owners are expected to act responsibly and clean up after their dogs.
- The main entrance is Philips Lane, Darrington, WF8 3BH.
- Bonfire Night takes place on Saturday 7 November 2026.
- Bonfire Night gates open at 5pm.

Ticketing, full timings, parking, detailed accessibility arrangements and safety guidance for Bonfire Night remain subject to event-team confirmation.

---

## 5. Launch gate

Public launch should not proceed until:

- the Privacy Notice and Accessibility Statement have been approved;
- requested amendments have been applied;
- final accessibility checks have been recorded;
- a controlled Netlify preview has been tested; and
- the trustees have authorised the public launch.
